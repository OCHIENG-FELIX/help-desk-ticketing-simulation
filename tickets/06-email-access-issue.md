# Ticket 006: Email Access Issue

## Ticket Information
- Ticket ID: HD-006
- Category: Email / Application
- Type: Incident
- Priority: P2 - High (Illustrative)
- Assigned Team: IT Support L1
- Status: Escalated (Simulated)

## User Report
A user cannot access their organizational email account.

## Initial Investigation
- Confirm whether the issue affects webmail, desktop email, or both.
- Check internet connectivity.
- Record the displayed error message.
- Verify account status through approved procedures.
- Check whether other users are affected.
- Investigate possible service disruptions.

## Possible Causes
- Incorrect account configuration.
- Account lockout.
- Authentication problems.
- Email service interruption.
- Network connectivity issues.

## Action Taken
The issue is escalated to the appropriate account or email support team if L1 checks cannot resolve it.

## Verification
Pending specialist investigation.

## Closure Notes
The ticket remains open until email access is restored and confirmed.

## Lessons Learned
Email troubleshooting requires distinguishing between device, network, account, and service-level problems.
