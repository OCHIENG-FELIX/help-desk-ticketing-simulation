# Ticket 006: Email Access Issue

## 1. Incident Details

- **Ticket ID:** HD-006
- **Category:** Email / Microsoft 365 Support
- **Priority:** P2 – High
- **Status:** Resolved (Simulated)
- **Operating System:** Windows 11
- **Application:** Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365

## 2. User Report

An employee contacted the IT Help Desk because they could not access their company email.

Whenever they attempted to sign in through Outlook, an error appeared indicating that their account or password was incorrect.

The employee needed to access an important email from their manager.

## 3. Initial Investigation

The following questions were asked:

- When was the last time you accessed your email successfully?
- What error message appears when you try to sign in?
- Which applications were you running before trying to access your email?
- Have you performed any recent activities or changes on your email account?

### Findings

- The employee accessed their email successfully the previous day.
- Outlook displayed: "Your account or password is incorrect."
- The employee had not been running any unusual applications.
- The employee changed their Microsoft 365 password the previous day.
- The employee had not tried signing in through a web browser.

## 4. Further Investigation

A follow-up question was asked:

**After changing your password yesterday, did you enter the new password when signing in to Outlook, or were you still using the old password?**

### Finding

The employee confirmed that they were still using the old password saved in Outlook and had not tried the new password.

## 5. Troubleshooting Steps

### Step 1: Identify the Password Change

Established that the employee had recently changed their Microsoft 365 password.

### Step 2: Guide the Employee

Advised the employee to enter their new password when prompted by Outlook.

The employee entered the password themselves. The IT Support Technician did not request or handle the employee's password.

### Step 3: Verify Account Access

Confirmed that:

- Outlook signed in successfully.
- The mailbox opened without an authentication error.
- The employee could access their email.

### Step 4: Test Email Functionality

With the employee's permission:

- Sent a test email.
- Confirmed that the employee received it.
- Asked the employee to reply to verify outgoing email functionality.

**Finding:** Sending and receiving emails worked successfully.

## 6. Root Cause Assessment

The immediate issue was caused by Outlook continuing to use the employee's previously saved password after a Microsoft 365 password change.

The employee had not updated the credentials being used for sign-in.

## 7. Resolution

The employee entered the new password and successfully accessed Outlook.

Email sending and receiving were also verified.

**Final Status:** Resolved (Simulated).

## 8. Security and Privacy Considerations

- Never ask employees to disclose their passwords.
- Allow employees to enter their own credentials.
- Protect confidential account information.
- Follow organizational authentication and password policies.
- Never store passwords in incident tickets.
